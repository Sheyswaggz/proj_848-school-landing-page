About Oakwood Academy

Founded in 1990, Oakwood Academy has established itself as a beacon of educational excellence in Springfield. Our mission is to cultivate curious, confident, and compassionate learners who are prepared to make meaningful contributions to our global society.

We believe education extends beyond textbooks and test scores. Our holistic approach integrates rigorous academics with character development, creative expression, and physical wellness. Through innovative teaching methods and a supportive community, we nurture each student's unique talents and aspirations.

Our state-of-the-art facilities, award-winning faculty, and comprehensive programs create an environment where students don't just learn—they thrive. From our science laboratories to our art studios, from our athletic fields to our music halls, every space at Oakwood is designed to inspire discovery and excellence.

Our Programs

Discover our comprehensive educational programs designed to inspire curiosity, foster creativity, and build excellence

🔬

Academic Excellence

Our rigorous academic program emphasizes STEM education, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills. Students engage with cutting-edge technology, advanced placement courses, and research opportunities that prepare them for top-tier universities.

  • Advanced STEM curriculum with robotics and coding
  • 15 AP courses and honors programs
  • Partnership with local universities for dual enrollment
  • Average SAT score: 1380 (top 10% nationally)
🎨

Creative Arts

Our comprehensive arts program nurtures creative expression through visual arts, music, theater, and digital media. Students explore their artistic talents in professional-grade studios and performance spaces under the guidance of accomplished artists.

  • Professional art studios and music practice rooms
  • Annual theater productions and art exhibitions
  • Digital media lab with video editing and animation
  • Scholarship opportunities for exceptional talent
⚽

Athletic Programs

Our championship athletic program promotes physical fitness, teamwork, and sportsmanship. With 18 varsity sports and state-of-the-art facilities, we develop well-rounded athletes who excel both on the field and in the classroom.

  • 18 varsity sports with championship-winning teams
  • Olympic-size pool and fitness center
  • Professional coaching staff and athletic trainers
  • 95% of athletes qualify for college programs
